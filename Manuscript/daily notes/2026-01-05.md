- **CDP-choline (citicoline)** is a neuroprotective compound that, when used for amblyopia, is thought to:

	**Proposed mechanisms:**

	- **Enhances neuroplasticity** - promotes neural connections and synaptic remodeling in the visual cortex
	- **Neuroprotection** - supports retinal ganglion cells and optic nerve function
	- **Neurotransmitter synthesis** - increases acetylcholine and dopamine, which are involved in visual processing
	- **Improves visual pathway function** - may enhance signal transmission from eye to brain

- The active video game10,14 presented different game elements to each eye. The amblyopic eye saw a subset of game elements at 100% contrast. The fellow eye saw remaining game elements at a lower contrast set individually using a dichoptic global motion measure of interocular suppression.20-22 Where participants could not reliably perform this test, fellow eye contrast was manually set to allow simultaneous perception of all game elements during binocular viewing. Binocular combination was required to successfully play the active video game. Fellow eye contrast increased proportionally by 15% each day if the game was played for at least 15 minutes and a high score of at least 1000 points was achieved in the previous day. The placebo game presented all game elements to both eyes at full contrast (no dichoptic presentation), simulating a normal video game.
